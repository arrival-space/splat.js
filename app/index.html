Splat.js

    
Gaussian splat training, in a browser tab

    
Give it overlapping photographs of a place and the whole pipeline runs here:
       landmarks are found and matched across the photos, the camera positions are
       solved for, those points become Gaussian splats, and the splats are trained
       against your photographs until the render matches them. What comes out is a
       standard .ply that opens in any splat viewer.

    

      Nothing is uploaded.
      No server, no cloud, no queue, no account. Your photographs are decoded
        in this page and never leave your machine — there is nowhere for them to be
        sent, and nothing is stored.
